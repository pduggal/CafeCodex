Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a café?
Swipe right on a café card or tap the heart icon to add it to your "Want to Go" list.

How do I mark a café as visited?
Swipe left on a café card or tap "Mark Visited" on the café detail page.

How do I recommend a café?
Go to the "Recommend" tab and fill out the nomination form. If your café makes the cut, you'll be credited as the recommender.

How do I delete my account?
Go to the Author tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

Is Café Codex free?
Yes, Café Codex is completely free with no in-app purchases.
